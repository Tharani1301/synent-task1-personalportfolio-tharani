Pet Care Recommendations

Daily handling tips to keep your pet healthy, calm & happy

🐕 Daily Routine Care

  • Feed pets at fixed times daily
  • Provide fresh water 24×7
  • Maintain a consistent walking schedule
  • Avoid sudden routine changes

🍖 Food Handling

  • Avoid chocolate, onion & spicy food
  • Control portions to prevent obesity
  • Use treats only for training
  • Do not overfeed out of affection

🩺 Home Health Check

  • Check eyes & ears weekly
  • Watch for itching or hair loss
  • Monitor appetite changes
  • Visit vet if symptoms appear

💉 Vaccination & Medicines

  • Never miss vaccination dates
  • Do not reuse old prescriptions
  • Never change medicine dosage
  • Consult vet before any medication

🐾 Behavior Management

  • Never shout or hit pets
  • Use calm voice & positive rewards
  • Give pets a safe resting space
  • Spend daily bonding time

🌦 Weather-Based Care

  • Summer: walk only morning/evening
  • Rainy: dry paws to prevent fungus
  • Winter: warm bedding for seniors
  • Hydration is critical in all seasons
